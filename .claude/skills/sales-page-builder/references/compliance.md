# Compliance and honesty rules

A sales page that lies gets refunds, chargebacks, ad-account bans and legal
risk (US FTC, EU consumer law, India Consumer Protection Act / ASCI). These
are not optional.

## Never invent
- Ratings, review counts, member/customer counts.
- Revenue, sales numbers, "X sales in Y days".
- Testimonials, names, faces, chat screenshots, payment dashboards.
- Scarcity ("only 3 left") or countdown timers that reset.
- "As seen on" logos the user hasn't actually appeared in.

If the brief has no proof yet: drop the proof sections and use
**process proof** instead (show the product working, a demo video, a free
sample page). Tell the user proof is the biggest gap.

## Always include
- A results disclaimer next to every income/result claim:
  "My own result, not typical." / "Individual results, not typical."
- Footer earnings disclaimer: "Results vary. We make no guarantee of income
  or sales. Examples shown are not typical."
- Refund/guarantee terms that match the real policy, linked in the footer.
- Trademark line when naming third-party tools: "Not affiliated with or
  endorsed by Anthropic, OpenAI, Meta…" (only the ones named).
- Price, currency and "one-time" vs "subscription" stated clearly.
- Links to Privacy, Terms, Refund policy, Contact.

## Brand safety
- Copy the structure, not the identity. No other brand's logo, product name,
  mascot, or copied sentences.
- Characters/mascots: generate original ones; don't use recognisable
  real people or copyrighted characters.
