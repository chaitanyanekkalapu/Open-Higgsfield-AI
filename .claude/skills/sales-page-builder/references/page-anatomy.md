# Page anatomy

Breakdown of the reference page (mobile screenshot), top to bottom, plus the
sections that normally follow below the fold on this kind of page.

Goal of the page: sell ONE low-price digital product ($5–$47 "tripwire") to
cold or warm traffic from social media, on a phone, in under 60 seconds.

## Above the fold (what the screenshot shows)

| # | Section | What it does | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Thin lime top bar | Brand accent, signals "this is a product page" | 3–4px, full width |
| 1 | Nav: logo + pill CTA "Get it, $4.97" | Price is visible from second 1; buy is always one tap away | Logo = bold word + italic serif word. CTA states price. No menu links (no exits). |
| 2 | Rating pill "★ 4.7 · 700+ builders in the community" | Instant social proof before the headline | Only real numbers. Lime badge for the score. |
| 3 | Headline, 3 lines, centered | The promise: result + "while you sleep" (passive benefit) | Heavy grotesk. ONE word in a lime box ("sell"). ONE word in italic serif ("sleep.") with a hand-drawn underline. Max ~8 words. |
| 4 | Product mockup | Shows the *output* (a real-looking page for a med spa), not the product itself | Browser frame with URL. Floating status chips ("✓ Headline written", "✓ Proof placed") = the AI doing work. Prompt bar with typing caret: "Build me a sales page for my med spa". Optional mascot character pointing at it (attention + personality). |
| 5 | Big CTA "Get The System, $4.97 →" | Primary action | Full width, lime, dark text, offset bottom shadow so it looks pressable. Price inside the button. |
| 6 | Risk reversal ticks | Removes the 3 main fears | "One payment" (no subscription) · "Instant download" (no waiting) · "30-day money back" (no risk) |
| 7 | Subhead / explainer | Says what it is in plain words | "The **<category>** that turns your **<tool>** into a **<result>**. Install in **2 minutes**. Describe your offer. *Watch it build.*" — bold nouns, underline the speed, italic the payoff. |
| 8 | Founder line | Trust: a real person + one big result | Round avatar with lime ring. "Built by <name>. One page built this way did **540+ sales in 90 days**. My own result, not typical." Disclaimer is part of the sentence. |
| 9 | Proof wall header + horizontal strip | Volume of proof | Label: "REAL SCREENSHOTS. MINE AND MY MEMBERS'. INDIVIDUAL RESULTS, NOT TYPICAL." Swipeable cards: dashboards, chat messages, reviews. |

## Below the fold (standard for this page type — build these too)

| # | Section | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | Problem / "Old way vs new way" | Name the pain: hiring designers, templates that don't convert, hours in page builders. Two columns or a before/after list. |
| 11 | How it works — 3 steps | Install → Describe your offer → Publish. Each step one line + small visual. Makes it feel easy. |
| 12 | What's inside (the bundle) | Each item: name, one-line benefit, (optional) "value $X". Real items only. |
| 13 | Who it's for / not for | Qualifies buyers, cuts refunds. 3–4 bullets each. |
| 14 | Testimonials (text) | Name, role/business, photo, specific result. Only real. |
| 15 | Price block | Crossed value stack (only if values are honest) → price → CTA → ticks. |
| 16 | Guarantee | Badge + plain terms ("30 days, email us, full refund, no questions"). Must match real policy. |
| 17 | FAQ | 5–8 objections: "Do I need to code?", "Which AI do I need?", "Is it a subscription?", "What if it doesn't work for my niche?", "How do I get it?" |
| 18 | Final CTA | Restate headline promise + button. |
| 19 | Sticky bottom bar (mobile) | Price + CTA, appears after the hero scrolls away. |
| 20 | Footer | Legal links, earnings/results disclaimer, "not affiliated with Anthropic/Meta/etc." where tools are named. |

## Why it works (techniques to reuse)

- **Price everywhere, low price** → no "how much?" friction; impulse buy.
- **Show the output, not the tool** → buyer imagines their own page.
- **Animated "AI is working" chips** → makes the automation tangible.
- **Niche example in the mockup** (med spa) → feels concrete; swap per audience.
- **Disclaimers baked into the sentence** → stays compliant without killing flow.
- **No navigation menu** → the only exit is the buy button.
- **Character/mascot** → stops the scroll on social traffic.
