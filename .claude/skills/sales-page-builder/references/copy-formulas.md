# Copy formulas

Write simple. Short words. One idea per line.

## Headline (pick one, max ~8 words, 3 lines on mobile)
1. **Result + passive benefit:** "Build <X> that <result> while you <easy thing>."
2. **Result + time:** "<Result> in <short time>. No <pain>."
3. **Who + result:** "The <niche> owner's shortcut to <result>."
4. **Old way killer:** "Stop <painful task>. Start <result>."

Mark ONE power word for the lime box (the verb of the result: *sell*, *book*,
*grow*). Mark ONE word for italic serif (the emotional payoff: *sleep*,
*fun*, *easy*). They must not be the same word.

## Subhead (2 lines)
"The **<category>** that turns your **<tool/asset>** into a **<outcome>**.
<Speed claim>. <Action>. *<Payoff>.*"

## CTA labels
- Nav: "Get it, <price>"
- Main: "Get The <Thing>, <price> →"
- Never: "Submit", "Click here", "Learn more".
Keep the same noun everywhere.

## Risk-reversal ticks (3, short)
"One payment" · "Instant download" · "<N>-day money back" — only if true.

## Founder line
"Built by <Name>. <One verifiable result>. My own result, not typical."

## Status chips in the mockup (the AI "working")
Past tense, 2–3 words: "Headline written", "Proof placed", "Offer stacked",
"Mobile checked", "Checkout linked".

## Prompt bar text
"Build me a sales page for my <niche>" — use the buyer's niche.

## How it works (3 steps)
1. **Install** — "<N> minutes. Copy, paste, done."
2. **Describe** — "Tell it your offer in one sentence."
3. **Publish** — "Get a page ready to take payments."

## FAQ — answer objections, not curiosities
Order: skill/effort → tools needed → price/subscription → fit for my niche →
delivery → refund.

## Words to avoid
"Revolutionary", "game-changing", "guaranteed income", "get rich",
"passive income guaranteed", any number not in the brief.
